# Finishing: start here (owned by the Finishing conversation)

Deadline **2026-11-28**: both arms working (fingers, LEDs) **and painted**. Owner plans a **full dry-fit before any glue**.

## Known so far (from the vault, 2026-11-22)
- Shells: LW-PLA, joined on wooden 6 × 30 dowels. Seams and thumb dowel: **UHU Plus Schnellfest**. Covers, lenses, electronics, buttons: **UHU Poly Max**. **No super glue near clear parts** (fogging).
- Clear parts: ring cover, hand lenses A/B, crystal, both pressure gauges (transparent PLA; **mask and paint the areas that should not glow**).
- Electronics install points that must happen **before** bonding over them (see [[LED_Wiring_Start_Here]]): strip double-wrapped in the ring trough and fed through the hand channel tunnels before the ring cover and lenses go on; sled glued under the ring (shell 08); button wires and USB-C routed along the inside; power bank on the cuff wall (Velcro). Keep strip pads off metal and off each other.
- Open: glue-up order per arm, sanding/filler, primer and paint system, masking plan, cure times, final assembly checklist.
