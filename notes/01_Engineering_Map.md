# Engineering map

## Hardware and goals
- Bambu Lab X1 Carbon final armor; A1 Mini may be used for small fit coupons.
- Original manually string-actuated fingers are retained; motorized knuckles deferred.
- Animated individually addressable LED effects and on-gauntlet button control are required.
- Serviceable LED cover, controller, battery, wiring and string conduits are design constraints.
