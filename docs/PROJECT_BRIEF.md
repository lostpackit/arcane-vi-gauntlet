# Project brief

## Objective
Reduce the number of large armor parts and visible seams in two Vi gauntlets while preserving functional original fingers, a serviceable circular lighting assembly, wearable controls, and access to repairs.

## Current build
- Printer: Bambu Lab X1 Carbon (0.4 mm nozzle).
- Original finger models from the supplied 3MF are being printed; do not alter mating geometry without measurements.
- Motorized knuckles deferred. Finger motion remains manual/string-actuated.
- LEDs, ESP32, switches, and power hardware not yet purchased or dimensionally verified.
- The smaller A1 Mini may be used for test coupons; final armor is targeted at the X1C.

## Version evolution
- V1: eight armor sections per arm; some cut geometry required repair.
- V2: mesh fixes and blind dowel socket prototypes; lighting and strings unresolved.
- V3: ring/cover fit coupons and proposed five PTFE conduits.
- V4: 13 sections per arm, ring retained in section 08, removable light cover concept, bonded seams and reinforcement components.

## Non-negotiable constraints
Keep 100% scale and original fingers compatible; check both hands, tendon travel and serviceability. Avoid irreversible holes or adhesives until electronics are chosen and fitted. Nominal build-volume compliance alone is insufficient to release a part.
