# Decisions and history

1. Use original string-operated fingers; defer moving/motorized knuckles.
2. Use animated addressable LEDs and on-gauntlet effect buttons rather than static lighting.
3. Consolidate large shells to reduce assembly work on the X1 Carbon.
4. V1 used eight sections per arm; V2 attempted mesh repair and dowel interfaces.
5. V3 focused on small fit coupons for ring/cover and PTFE string corridors.
6. V4 adopted 13 armor sections per arm, ring in section 08, covers and reinforced bonded seams.

## Risks
- V4 adhesive seams are not generally reversible.
- Ring fit, electronics placement, and finger/string routing are not validated.
- 13 sections per arm have not been shown to save total print/assembly time.
- Fused STL geometry may differ from the original multi-object 3MF.
- A digital watertight report does not establish physical strength or printability.

## Next steps
Independent CAD audit; select LED and control hardware; Bambu Studio slicing; small fit/strength coupons; revise as V5 if needed; then full acceptance.
